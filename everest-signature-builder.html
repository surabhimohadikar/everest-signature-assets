How to add it to your email
  1. Fill in your name, title and phone, then click Copy signature.
  2. Gmail: Settings (gear) › See all settings › General › Signature › Create new. Name it “Business Show”, then paste into the box and click Save Changes.
  3. Outlook (desktop): File › Options › Mail › Signatures › New. Paste into the box and click OK.
  4. Outlook (web): Settings (gear) › Accounts › Signatures › New signature. Paste and Save.
  5. Send yourself a test email and click the banner to make sure it opens the landing page.

Images need an internet connection to show. After the show (26 Nov), switch back to your usual signature.
